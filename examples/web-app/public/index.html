Credential Lifecycle Management: The "Revoke & Supersede" Pattern

Long-Term Validity Architecture

Because cryptographic signatures are mathematically bound to specific data and blockchain history is immutable, credentials cannot be "edited in-place." Instead, ScatterID uses the industry-standard Revoke & Supersede pattern: a new Version 2 credential is generated, cryptographically linked to Version 1 via replacesCredentialId, and Version 1 is marked revoked on the Fabric ledger.
